# MAGESHIP — Minimalist Luxury Clothing Brand & Custom Atelier

A web application designed for **Mageship**, featuring a modern **all-white luxury aesthetic**, high-density typography, an interactive **T-Shirt Customizer Studio**, and full product collections.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Requirements

### 1. Color Coding: Pure White Luxury Theme
- **Color Palette**: Dominant pure white (`#FFFFFF`), subtle alabaster and snow layers (`#FAFAFA`, `#F4F5F7`), delicate structural borders (`#E5E7EB`), and high-contrast obsidian black accents (`#111827`).
- **Typography**: Dual modern typefaces — `Space Grotesk` for architectural editorial headlines and `Plus Jakarta Sans` for body text.

### 2. Home Page
- **Hero Section**: Architectural showcase featuring the Summer Capsule '26, 280 GSM heavyweight organic combed cotton highlights, trust badges (GOTS certified, 30-day exchange), and rating proof.
- **Custom T-Shirt Feature Spotlight**: Dedicated creative atelier banner showcasing the bespoke T-shirt design options, live preview mockup, and quick-launch buttons.
- **Best Selling Products Grid**: Filterable grid (T-Shirts, Hoodies & Knitwear, Trousers & Shorts) with instant quick-view modals, direct-add to bag, and wishlist toggles.
- **Brand Pillars & Verified Community Reviews**: Real customer testimonials and core brand values.

### 3. Categories & Navigation
- **Men's Collection**: Heavyweight dropped-shoulder tees, studio pullover hoodies, tailored linen overshirts, pleated trousers, and cargo shorts with filtering and sorting (Price, Rating, Featured).
- **Women's Collection**: Sculpted cropped tees, silk-cotton knit cardigans, fluid slip midi dresses, tailored high-waisted trousers, and resort sets.
- **Contact Us**:
  - Atelier location & showroom info in Soho, New York.
  - Dedicated concierge emails for custom bulk orders and general inquiries.
  - Interactive dispatch form with live client-side validation.
  - Interactive FAQ accordion covering custom garment lead times, bulk ordering, and cotton care.

### 4. Interactive T-Shirt Customizer Studio
- **Garment Silhouette & Hue**: Switch between Heavy Boxy Fit (280 GSM), Classic Fit (220 GSM), and Cropped Boxy (240 GSM). Choose fabric shades (Pure White, Alabaster, Oatmeal, Ice Glacier, Charcoal, Obsidian).
- **Live 360° Angle**: Toggle between Front View and Back View.
- **Typography Studio**: Real-time custom text input, font family selector (Space Grotesk, Jakarta, Typewriter, Editorial Serif, Headline), dynamic font size slider, position selector (Center Chest, Pocket, Oversized, Collar), and text color swatches.
- **Artwork & Logo Upload**: Choose from studio preset badges (Geometry, M-Emblem, Barcode Archive, Botanical, North Star) or drag-and-drop your own custom logo/image file with live canvas placement.
- **Dynamic Pricing Engine & Bag Integration**: Automatically updates pricing with garment tier and high-density print fees, and exports directly to the shopping cart drawer.

### 5. Shopping Bag, Wishlist & Search
- **Cart Slide-Over Drawer**: Real-time quantity modifier, item removal, free shipping tracker bar ($75 threshold), and promo code verification (try code `WHITE15` or `FREESHIP`).
- **Wishlist Drawer**: Save favorite pieces across sessions.
- **Instant Search Modal**: Search across catalog titles, fabric weights, and categories with trending search tags.
